Welcome to First Smile Baby World

Located in the heart of Gandhinagar at Setu Square, First Smile Baby World is more than just a store; it's a premium boutique designed to make parenting a beautiful journey.

We understand that every parent wants the absolute best for their child. That's why we meticulously curate our collection, focusing on safety, comfort, quality, and style. From the softest newborn apparel to educational toys and sturdy strollers, we house trusted brands and exclusive designs for children aged 0 to 5 years.
